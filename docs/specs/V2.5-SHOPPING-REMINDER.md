# V2.5 weekly ingredient reminder

This contract supersedes V1 F07 on `implementation/v2`. The navigation and page title remain `Llista de la compra`. The page explains its purpose with `Ingredients necessaris per al menú setmanal` and uses the same dated week navigation as the planner. `/compra/` opens the current Madrid week and `/compra/<week>/` opens the requested week. Both support GET and HEAD. Former mutation and form routes return 404. Other methods on the two valid routes are refused.

## Derivation and completeness

The application reads the stored week and its slots together in a short transaction. Each selected occurrence contributes its saved effective recipe for two adults, including an archived dish still selected in the plan. Pending and skipped slots, live template suggestions and missing weeks contribute no quantities. The presentation distinguishes those states. Menu and recipe commands commit their editable snapshots as before. The next reminder read projects those snapshots without writing or caching a separate shopping record.

Ingredient identity remains the recipe's canonical Unicode name key. Group amounts by identity and compatible family, using exact Decimal operations and existing conversions to grams, millilitres and count. Incompatible families and distinct ingredient forms have separate rows. Names, notes and `Al gust` are preserved. A named ingredient with a missing amount appears as `Quantitat pendent`. When the unit family is known, an accompanying quantified subtotal is shown only for that family. When the unit is unknown, its row stays separate and numeric amounts for the same ingredient are marked as known subtotals. First-draft amounts are explicitly provisional. A later draft takes effect only on publication. Missing recipes and first drafts flag the selected dish and meal, so the reminder does not imply full coverage.

Shop sections are presentation rules over a small exact-name vocabulary. They appear in the order `Fruita i verdura`, `Carn`, `Peix`, `Lactis i ous`, `Rebost`, then `Altres`. Unrecognized ingredients fall under `Altres`. Section lookup does not alter canonical identities or join separate ingredient rows. The reminder includes recipe staples and makes no stock assumptions.

## Historical and legacy compatibility

The existing `shopping_projection_version=1` and slot snapshot schema stay in place. Historical totals still derive solely from frozen effective recipes and their exact base quantities. The V2.5 presentation displays incompleteness and groups ingredients while retiring the legacy adjustment layer. A selected historical dish with recorded recipe status `missing` is identified as unavailable. If the stored week cannot be safely decoded, the reminder returns a localized unavailable screen and uses no live recipe fallback. Neither path rewrites history.

V2.5 has no database migration. Legacy manual items, overrides, checked state and receipts stay in their original tables. The reminder and menu edits leave them untouched. A backup taken from the V2.4 schema remains compatible. Preserve that data for rollback and audit.

Use the private owner fixture supplied with the handoff. It contains the verified catalogue and media plus a sample historical menu and an editable menu with two occurrences of the same dish and one skipped meal. Keep `menu.sqlite3` and `recipe-photos/` together. Start each review from a new copy of the fixture. Review on a phone and in tablet landscape, and repeat the navigation and photo-fallback checks with JavaScript disabled.

1. Open `/plats/` and review the new photographs against their dishes, including a newly added photo and one of the original nine. Open dishes 16, 51, 52, 56 and 57. Check that the ingredients and preparation remain usable and that each page omits the photo area.
2. Open `/compra/` and navigate to the populated weeks listed in the handoff. Compare the historical single occurrence and the editable pair. Check the doubled quantities, supermarket sections, omitted-meal message and read-only shopping controls. Follow `Ves al menú setmanal`, then remove or replace one occurrence and check that only its contribution changes. Open an empty week.
3. In the QA copy, create a test dish and plan it before adding a recipe. Check `Recepta pendent`. Save a first draft with a measured ingredient and a named ingredient without an amount. Check `Receptes incompletes`, `Provisional` and `Quantitat pendent`. Supply the missing data, publish, and verify the next reminder.
4. Change one quantity in the repeated dish’s recipe and publish on the QA copy. Check the editable reminder and confirm the historical quantity and recipe stay fixed. Review the fallback section `Altres` and a long ingredient name for horizontal overflow.
5. Check that the same photo still loads after restarting the QA process or container. Record one combined owner decision before release. Verify household network isolation separately before any household rollout.

## One owner review on a disposable copy

Copy the synthetic fixture supplied with the handoff into a new temporary directory. From this checkout, start a loopback-only development server using that copy.

```sh
v25_owner_dir=$(mktemp -d /tmp/menu-v25-owner.XXXXXXXX)
cp /absolute/path/to/synthetic-qa/menu.sqlite3 "$v25_owner_dir/menu.sqlite3"
DJANGO_ENV=development DJANGO_DEBUG=0 DJANGO_ALLOWED_HOSTS=127.0.0.1 \
  DJANGO_DB_PATH="$v25_owner_dir/menu.sqlite3" \
  .venv/bin/python manage.py runserver 127.0.0.1:18765 --noreload
```

1. Open `http://127.0.0.1:18765/compra/`. Check the `Llista de la compra` label, the explanatory subheading, dated week navigation, supermarket sections and absence of mutation controls. The fixture also includes a populated week beginning 23 November 2026. Compare its six sections with the supplied phone and landscape captures.
2. Follow `Ves al menú setmanal`. On an editable week, plan the same dish twice, then replace or omit one occurrence. Return to the reminder each time and check the totals. Open an empty week and a week with an omitted meal.
3. On the disposable copy, save a first draft with one measured ingredient and one named ingredient without an amount. Select the dish and check `Provisional` and `Quantitat pendent`. Publish the recipe and check the updated quantities. Open a previous week and verify that its recorded recipes and totals stay fixed.
4. Review the fallback section `Altres`, scroll through a long ingredient name in portrait and landscape, and repeat week navigation with JavaScript off. Record the combined owner decision before release.

Historical coverage is limited to the existing saved recipe snapshots. Group classification uses exact recognized names with `Altres` for the rest. The reminder expresses ingredients required by the selected dishes. Household stock is outside its data.
